🌍 What Is SDG 14?

SDG 14: Life Below Water is one of the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals. It aims to conserve and sustainably use oceans, seas and marine resources.

Oceans provide food, support biodiversity, create jobs and help regulate Earth's climate. Protecting them is important for people, animals and future generations.
